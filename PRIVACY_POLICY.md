# Privacy Policy for Clicka

**Last updated:** July 12, 2026

## Introduction

Clicka ("we", "our", or "the app") is an automated touch/click automation tool built for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**Clicka does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

The app operates entirely on-device. No data is sent to external servers, third-party services, or any cloud infrastructure.

---

## 2. Accessibility Service Usage

Clicka uses Android's **Accessibility Service** to perform automated touch gestures and swipe actions on your screen. This is the core functionality of the app.

- The Accessibility Service is used **solely** to simulate touch events (taps and swipes) at coordinates defined by the user.
- We do **not** read, log, capture, or transmit any content from other apps on your screen.
- We do **not** retrieve window content, text, or any data from applications you use.
- The service is only active when explicitly enabled by the user.

---

## 3. Data Storage

Clicka stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

- User-configured click/swipe positions and settings (saved using a local Room database).
- App preferences such as selected mode and configuration.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

---

## 4. Permissions

Clicka requests the following permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE` | Required to perform automated touch gestures on screen |
| `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW` | Required to display the overlay control panel above other apps |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | Required to keep the service running while automating gestures |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Optional – allows the service to restore state after device restart |

No permissions are used to collect or transmit user data.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

Clicka does **not** integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or data collection services.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Clicka does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages.

---
